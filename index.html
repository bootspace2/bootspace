The Gap

India has car buyers.
We're building for car lovers.

01

No Cultural Home

Cars24, CarDekho, CarWale — great for buying and selling. But not one platform exists for the enthusiast who wants to modify, showcase, or just talk cars with people who get it.

02

Hidden Merchants

The best tuners, modifiers, and custom workshops in India have no digital storefront. Word-of-mouth shouldn't be the only way to find someone who can build your dream build.

03

Scattered Events

Car meets, drag events, auto expos happen across India every weekend — but there's no single place to discover, join, or organize them. The community is fragmented.

04

No Showcase Layer

You spent lakhs building your car. Where do you show it off? Instagram is not built for car culture. There's no platform that understands what a build thread, a spec sheet, or a garage profile means.
